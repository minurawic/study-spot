ABOUT STUDYSPOT

Helping Sri Lankan students find a place to focus

StudySpot started with a simple problem: many students live in boarding houses where quiet, comfortable study space is hard to find. We map libraries, cafés, co-working spaces and university areas so you can find, book and review the right spot in minutes.

How StudySpot works

1. Discover

Search by type, location, Wi-Fi and noise level, or open the map to see spots near you.

2. Book

Pick a date and time, choose how many people, and confirm your spot in a few taps.

3. Review

Share how it went so other students know which places are truly quiet and worth the trip.

What we care about

Students first

Honest reviews

Ratings come from real bookings so you can trust what you read.

Local focus
